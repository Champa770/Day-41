Why Start with HTML & CSS?

HTML is the skeleton of every website. It gives structure to your content, like headings, paragraphs, and images. Without HTML, the web wouldn’t exist the way we know it today.

CSS adds style and design to that structure. Together, they are the foundation for any web developer. Mastering them first makes learning JavaScript much easier later.

Top 2 Tips for Beginners

Tip 1: Practice daily, even if it’s just 30 minutes. Consistency beats intensity when learning to code.

Tip 2: Build small projects instead of just watching tutorials. Try recreating simple pages like a portfolio or contact form.
